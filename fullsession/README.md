# FullSession

See how visitors really use your website, right from Claude. Ask about frustrated pages, funnel drop-offs or specific sessions, and get clear answers with charts and links to the session replays.

- Find where visitors struggle and why: pages and elements with rage, dead and error clicks
- See where your funnels lose people, and who dropped
- Find and replay sessions
- Track trends, read your dashboards and get AI recommendations on what to fix

## Use it

Connect the FullSession connector and sign in with your FullSession account. Then ask in plain language, for example:

- "Which pages have the most rage clicks this week?"
- "Where do users drop off in my checkout funnel?"
- "Show me recent sessions with JavaScript errors."

The plugin's skills guide Claude through common investigations, such as tracing frustration from a page down to the element and the session replays behind it.

## Data

The plugin connects to FullSession's MCP server at `https://app.fullsession.io/mcp` using your own FullSession sign-in. Claude sends FullSession the data requests needed to answer your questions (such as a site, a time range or a funnel) and receives analytics and session information from the sites your account can access. When you ask Claude to draft a segment or funnel, your description is sent to FullSession to create the draft. Creating, changing or deleting items happens only when you ask, and Claude asks you to confirm first. The plugin stores nothing itself. See FullSession's [privacy policy](https://www.fullsession.io/privacy-policy).
